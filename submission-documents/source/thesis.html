REBRAND THESIS / A MORE CONSIDERED CREATIVE PARTNER

Turn long videos
into standout clips.

Keep the familiar promise. Make the creative decisions behind the output more visible.

OpusClip’s current homepage leads with “1 long video, 10 viral clips” and “Create 10x faster,” supported by a broad catalogue of capabilities. That emphasis communicates speed and output. Our direction presents a calmer editing partner: finding moments worth sharing, giving them a distinct visual form, and keeping creators close to the decisions that matter.

The principle: make the value visible.

Swiss editorial discipline meets a film contact sheet. Warm-white space, near-black Instrument Sans, restrained violet, precise alignment and discreet timecodes give every visual a job: show the source, the crop, the caption treatment, or the question a clip might answer. Motion explains a relationship; it does not decorate the page.

Why this fits OpusClip.

OpusClip’s homepage states it is used by 20M+ creators and businesses. The direction must feel approachable to an individual and credible to a team. One consistent product-led system can explain clipping, captions and reframing without renaming the company. The official logo stays the point of recognition.

Preserve the video-to-clips promise; give the material more space. Redesign imagery is a labeled licensed stock-still composition, not generated video or spoken quotations.

ONE HOMEPAGE / ONE FOCUSED ADDITION

A creative partner.
An inspectable result.

Core product first.

Approximately 70% of the page’s emphasis stays with core OpusClip: link entry, a source-to-clips composition, a compact workflow, creator/team relevance and a closing action. A distinct charcoal evidence stage introduces audience-informed clipping without becoming a separate research brand.

A question becomes a candidate clip.

Audience research discovers supported questions. OpusClip generates candidates after an explicit credit acknowledgment. A separate review agent evaluates the returned clips; ordinary application code coordinates the handoff and retains job IDs. Comments, source passages, classifications and caveats remain visible. Partial answers and no-match outcomes are legitimate results—not gaps to fill with invented certainty.

A buildable system.

Reusable grids, typography, buttons and media frames map to Webflow. An embedded application owns the workbench. A small animation layer connects evidence; future Rive may animate only the marker. Text, controls and playback stay semantic HTML.

What we would evaluate.

Time to the first creator-approved clip, sessions reaching that point, and creator agreement that the clip answers the cited question. These are proposed evaluation criteria, not measured improvements.
