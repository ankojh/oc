REBRAND THESIS / MORE FROM EVERY MOMENT

Clarity over noise.
Craft over hype.

A calmer OpusClip homepage that makes long video → short clips immediately legible, then gives audience usefulness a place in the workflow.

01 / KEEP THE CORE PROMISE

One video. New possibilities.

The official homepage leads with “1 long video, 10 viral clips,” speed, creator proof and a wide feature catalogue. This concept preserves link-first clipping, reframing, captions and the official identity, but makes the promise more precise: turn long videos into standout clips. One landscape source and two portrait cuts explain the product without motion or a feature tour.

02 / THE AUDIENCE FIT

Creators need a clear way to repurpose existing work. Teams need consistent visual expression. Editorial typography and real crop proportions signal creative control without another decorative dashboard.

03 / A MEASURED ADDITION

Approximately 70% core product, 30% concept feature. Audience questions inform clips; they are not virality predictions. Original comments, source passages, missing context and no-match outcomes make usefulness inspectable.

Alignment, typography, source framing and small timeline details replace gradients, glass and a grid of identical feature cards.

04 / DESIGN LANGUAGE

Warm white, near-black, violet actions and charcoal evidence stages. Instrument Sans supplies the hierarchy; sparse monospace labels communicate indices and source time. A 1200px twelve-column desktop grid becomes a genuine single-column mobile layout with 20px gutters.

05 / BUILD FEASIBILITY

Semantic HTML/CSS, self-hosted fonts and a small animation layer. The focused workbench uses the existing coordinator’s real routes, not a third agent. Research is separate from credit-acknowledged Generate. Refresh observes a saved ID; retries retain the same clips and evidence.

06 / TRUST AS A DESIGN DETAIL

Show what the evidence can say.

The sample includes two partial answers, an unanswered question and a clip with unavailable transcript/ranges. No forced recommendations. Questions and quotations come from existing synthetic fixtures. The hero is a licensed stock-still composition; its captions are illustrative, not spoken quotations. Team controls link to the official product rather than pretending the prototype implements administration.

A homepage first. A useful concept second.

The signature question-to-clip connection settles in ~600ms, updates native accessible state immediately, cancels stale transitions and has a no-motion alternative. Webflow can host the editorial sections; an embedded application owns evidence and video. Rive may animate the marker later, but never contains the evidence or controls.
